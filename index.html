Features

Everything your home needs to know.

One small hub connects your lights, heating, locks and cameras, then quietly learns how your family lives.

Adaptive climate

Haven learns the temperature each person likes and adjusts room by room, before anyone walks in.

Lighting that follows you

Lights warm up as you enter a room and dim as you leave. Haven always knows which room you're in.

Presence awareness

See who's home at a glance. Haven recognises every person in the household by the sound of their footsteps.

Natural voice

Talk to Haven like a person. It remembers everything you have ever said to it, and in which room.

Total security
